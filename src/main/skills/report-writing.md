# Report writing

Lead with the outcome. The reader should know in one line whether the goal is met.

Structure:
1. **Result** — one or two sentences: what now exists or works.
2. **Delivered** — files, folders or documents, with paths.
3. **How it was verified** — the commands run and their real output (test counts, build status). If something was not verified, say so.
4. **Decisions** — choices you made that the reader may want to change.
5. **Open issues** — known gaps, risks, next steps. Write "none" if there are none.

Rules: plain language, short sentences, no filler or praise. Numbers over adjectives. Never claim something works without having checked it.
